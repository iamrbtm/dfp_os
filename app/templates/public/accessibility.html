Accessibility Commitment

Accessibility

Dude Fish Printing wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including customers using assistive technology, keyboard navigation, zoom, or alternative input methods.

How we approach accessibility

We aim to use clear headings, readable color contrast, descriptive form labels, and straightforward navigation across the public storefront and customer-contact pages.

We also try to keep checkout, contact, and custom-order flows understandable without requiring specialized technical knowledge about 3D printing.

Known limits

Some pages may still contain areas that need improvement, especially where new features, product media, or third-party services are involved. We treat accessibility fixes as product work, not cosmetic extras.

Contact Us for Accessibility Help

If you have trouble using this site, need help placing an order, or want information in a different format, contact us through the contact page.

Please include the page you were using, what you were trying to do, and any device or browser details that may help us reproduce the issue.
